AutoCare

Know when your car needs service, and keep its whole history in your pocket.

AutoCare is a free car maintenance tracker. It fills in the factory maintenance schedule for your car, reminds you before a service or a document expires, and tracks fuel economy and costs.

  • Log a whole garage visit at once, with the cost of each part.
  • Fuel economy, cost per km and monthly spending.
  • Insurance and registration papers with expiry reminders.
  • A PDF service report, useful when you sell the car.
  • Fully in English and Arabic.

No ads, no account, and it works offline. Your data stays on your phone unless you back it up to your own Google Drive.
